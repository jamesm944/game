Grand RTS Synthesis · Built from First Principles

E · Imperium & Chronicle

The complete synthesis of Tactical RTS (AoE4/CoH3), Grand Strategy (Victoria 3/EU5/CK3), Settlement Survival (Manor Lords/Anno), and Colony Sims (RimWorld/Dwarf Fortress).

  • Macro Pulse Top Bar (Vic3 + EU5): 7 resources with production deltas (+/- per min), market inflation, Imperial Favor, Town Council Approval, Unrest risk %, and Pop counts by class strata (Slaves, Peasants, Craftsmen, Burghers, Nobility).
  • Left Operational Lenses (Vic3): 5 slide-out drawers (War, Civic & Tax, Council, Market, Deeds) keeping the playfield completely clear when not needed.
  • Adaptive Bottom Deck: Switches contextually between CK3/DF Pop character sheet (portrait, traits, household, mood thoughts, practice/rust skills), AoE4 Military tactical grid (12 commands, stances, formations, counter bonuses), and Manor Lords Workplace production (recipes, foreman, logistics).
  • Paradox Outliner (Right Rail): Pinned alerts for idle villagers (1-click cycle), raid warnings, council votes, and wonder progress.

One bar, one deck

AoE4 · AoE2 DE · AoM Retold

A single resource line on top and a single command deck on the bottom. Everything else is contextual. This is D's skeleton.

Edge-hugging, no rails

Company of Heroes 3 · Northgard

Tactical map, unit cards and alerts pinned to the corners. The playfield is never framed by full-height panels.

Panel-first sim depth

Manor Lords · Against the Storm · Frostpunk 2

Mood, needs, production and factions live in slide-in panels or one side column, opened on demand. The map stays readable.

Character-first right rail

TW: THREE KINGDOMS · TW: WARHAMMER III

Portraits, relationships and retinues get a dedicated right-hand surface. Good model for a People tab that is more than a list.

Societal lenses & Pop strata

Victoria 3 · EU5 · Crusader Kings III

State capacities in top bar, operational lenses (Production, Politics, Military) on left, Pop strata/classes, and character sheets. Separates macro governance from tactical unit control.

Age of Empires IV

Relic / World's Edge · 2021

Steal this: D's direct model: one 60px resource bar, one bottom deck (minimap · command grid · queue · inspector), left panel only on demand. Resources read as a single scan line.

Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition

Forgotten Empires · 2019

Steal this: The canonical deck geometry: minimap bottom-left, command card bottom-centre, selection bottom-right. Idle-villager and age-up affordances never move.

Age of Mythology: Retold

World's Edge · 2024

Steal this: Same skeleton as AoE2, but god powers and myth units get bigger, more legible tiles. Shows how 12 command tiles can read as abilities, not buttons.

Company of Heroes 3

Relic · 2023

Steal this: Tactical map top-left, VP/resource ticker top-right, unit cards bottom-centre with veterancy pips. Almost no chrome over the battlefield; the HUD hugs the edges.

Total War: WARHAMMER III

Creative Assembly · 2022

Steal this: Campaign and battle use different HUDs. Battle puts unit cards bottom, selected unit right, minimap bottom-right. Heavy ornament, always outside the play area.

Manor Lords

Slavic Magic · 2024

Steal this: Diegetic and almost HUD-less. Building and people panels slide in from the right; approval, treasury and population are small top-left tokens. Sim depth lives in panels, not rails.

Anno 1800

Blue Byte · 2019

Steal this: Bottom-centre build menu with a category ring, minimap bottom-left, goods in a top ribbon. Dense production chains stay in one horizontal band.

Frostpunk 2

11 bit studios · 2024

Steal this: City vitals top-left, heat/timeline bottom, faction council left. Stark monochrome plus one accent; the HUD reads as a diegetic instrument panel.

Against the Storm

Eremite Games · 2023

Steal this: Best modern run-based city-builder HUD: resource strip on top, settlement panel left, orders/glades right, build bar bottom. Every corner has exactly one job.

Northgard

Shiro Games · 2018

Steal this: Compact, console-friendly HUD: clan vitals top-left, objectives top-right, minimap bottom-left, selection bottom-right. Very little screen coverage.

They Are Billions

Numantian Games · 2019

Steal this: Survival-RTS HUD: colony stats top-left, wave timer top-centre, build grid bottom-centre, minimap bottom-left. Threat is the loudest element on screen.

Warcraft III: Reforged

Blizzard · 2020

Steal this: Hero-centric deck: hero portrait and inventory bottom-left, command card bottom-right, unit cards centre. Shows how one unit can own a whole panel.

Total War: THREE KINGDOMS

Creative Assembly · 2019

Steal this: Character-first campaign UI: portraits, relationships and retinues in a right rail; battle deployment is a full-screen board. Good model for the People and Victory tabs.

Victoria 3

Paradox Development Studio · 2022

Steal this: The definitive Pop and macro-economics HUD: state capacities and balance deltas in top bar, left lens system (Production, Politics, Diplomacy, Military), Pop strata (Lower, Middle, Upper) with Standard of Living, radical/loyalist split, and market goods pricing.

Crusader Kings III

Paradox Development Studio · 2020

Steal this: Character-first RPG strategy HUD: bottom-left character window with 3D portrait, traits, stress, health and opinions; top-left realm & council management; right-rail outliner; character interaction ring.

EU5 / Project Caesar

Paradox Tinto · Tinto Talks

Steal this: Demographic simulation ground-truth: location-level Pops (Nobles, Clergy, Burghers, Peasants, Slaves), Estates influence & satisfaction, Market centers with raw-to-finished goods pricing, food supply/starvation, Control & Proximity, Cabinet ministers with state missions, and parliamentary negotiations.

Core Architectural Insights for our RTS + Deep Sim:

  • Pops as First-Class Citizens: Pops aren't abstract statistics; they reside in physical locations, belong to social strata, consume food and goods, and pay taxes based on local Control.
  • Estates & Town Council: Different social classes have distinct political leverage. Taxing peasant job income or burgher trade without representation sparks unrest, petitions, or rebellion.
  • Markets & Logistics: Food and commodities flow through physical nodes. Siting a granary, mill, or company branch matters because local market access dictates prices and starvation buffers.
  • Cabinet / Delegation: Instead of micro-managing every single citizen manually, player appoints trusted ministers (Foremen, Managers, Councilors) with policy priorities.
